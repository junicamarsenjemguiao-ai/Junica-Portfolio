01 / About

Where business analysis meets user experience.

I'm a BS Information Technology student at Pampanga State University, working in the space where business analysis meets UI/UX design. My process starts with people and problems: gathering requirements, running competitive and feature research, and shaping everything into product backlogs and documentation that teams can actually build from.

I wireframe and prototype user-centered interfaces in Figma and Webflow, then check them against real usability needs. As a Business Analyst Intern at Twala (Ohelio Inc.), I keep product documentation up to date and prepare feature comparison research that helps the team make informed decisions, always working side by side with developers, designers, and product people.
